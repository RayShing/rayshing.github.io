Introduction

I joined Amazon as an Applied Scientist in July 2026. Previously, I worked at Meta Platforms, Inc. from July 2025 to June 2026. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science in 2026 and my B.S. in Computer Science in 2021.

I was trained as a systems researcher. During my Ph.D., I published 25 papers in systems venues, including 15 papers at top conferences as defined by CSRankings. Among them, 14 are first-author papers, including 8 at top conferences. I spent the final year of my Ph.D. at Meta, where I published four papers, including two at top-tier conferences, and contributed to internal product improvements driven by my research efforts. As of August 2026, my publications have received 981 citations, including 864 citations to my first-author work.

I am now focused on translating research insights into real-world product impact by addressing bottlenecks in large-scale AI systems deployed globally. Many of the most important challenges emerge only when systems operate at massive scale. As publications are no longer a primary focus of my career, this website will be updated less frequently.
